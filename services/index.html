Bundle services into one visit

Most of the properties we look after need more than a mow. A typical regular visit in North Lakes or Redcliffe covers the lawn, the edges, the hedges and a quick tidy of the garden beds, and a seasonal lawn treatment or a bit of tree trimming can be added when it's needed. Booking it all with one crew means one quote, one invoice and no waiting on three different tradies.
