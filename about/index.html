About Regal Lawns And Gardens

A local lawn mowing and garden maintenance business based in Deception Bay, servicing 22 suburbs across Moreton Bay and Brisbane's northside.

Local, and based where we work

Regal Lawns And Gardens PTY LTD is run by Ben Butler from Deception Bay. Being based here rather than across town means we're a short drive from every suburb we service, whether that's the Redcliffe peninsula to the east, North Lakes and Kallangur to the south or the Burpengary and Caboolture corridor to the north.

We do lawn mowing, acreage mowing and slashing, garden maintenance, hedge trimming, lawn treatments and weed control, and tree trimming, for homeowners, landlords and property managers, body corporates and commercial sites. Most clients use us for more than one of those, on a regular schedule, so their whole yard is handled by one crew on one visit.

How we work

  • Quoted before we start. You'll know the price up front, whether it's a one-off clean-up or an ongoing schedule.
  • Our own equipment. Mowers, ride-ons, trimmers and pole saws, with the green waste taken away afterwards.
  • Straight answers. If a tree needs an arborist or a lawn needs a treatment programme rather than a quick spray, we'll say so.
